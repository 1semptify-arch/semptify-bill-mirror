BILL LIVE DRAFT — working text

The draft skeleton lives in the outreach kit. This file becomes the working statute text once a private party's read lands — their fixes land here, then it goes to the Revisor's office via whichever member carries it.

Locked clauses (non-negotiable)

1. No subrogation — paid claim ends the matter

2. Landlord-paid → see RESOLVED 10/18+10/23 below: deposit-float + M1PR advance + landlord matched share fund the premium; landlord cost survives as the float they no longer hold. Mandatory stands. Rating = program base + experience surcharge (resolved 10/23).

3. Independent claims adjudication + tenant counter-claim lane

4. Tenant/consumer board seats

5. Manufactured-claim penalties

6. Public claims registry

7. Sunset + year-3 review

8. Renter's belongings coverage — contents insurance as a third pool leg (added 10/18; cap/deductible/opt-in-vs-mandatory = open drafting questions)

Open drafting questions (for sponsors / staff)

  • Administering agency: a private party or a private party? (the pilot runs at a private party regardless)
  • Hardship trigger list + coverage term (2–6 month range, needs actuarial)
  • Interaction with FHPAP emergency assistance — avoid duplication
  • Premium rate cap vs. market-set with oversight?
  • Split ratio between credit advance and landlord match — rechecked

10/23: base ≈ net float ~$3.25/mo + ~$6 renter-credit + ~$6 landlord match ≈ $15.33/mo at $1,300 rent. Net float = 3% of escrowed deposits (4% yield less the 1% statutory interest paid to the tenant — the earlier $4.33 figure counted the tenant's own 1%). Float is now an ONGOING leg (custodial duration), not year-1-only — year-1 ramps at ~half steady-state over the 12-month migration (seed option: payable premium advance, UI trust-fund precedent). Three-leg ≈ ~$30–35/mo pushes credit headroom. Belongings opt-in-vs-mandatory is the swing variable.

  • Remedy path: administrative appeal → appeal body and review standard still open.
  • Carrier-abuse controls (10/19 — "can they bribe the insurer into criminal charges?")

Private parties can't FILE charges, but they can refer fraud to prosecutors — needs (a) a penalty for knowingly-false fraud reports, symmetric to manufactured-claim penalties (probably lives inside clause 5), (b) tenant notice + defense window before a carrier transmits, (c) a defined evidence threshold. Corruption can't be designed to zero — design for visible (registry), expensive (penalties), appealable (independent review).

RESOLVED 10/18, AMENDED 10/19 + 10/23 — funding source (three-source braid)

Deposit → bonded escrow at signing, held for the tenancy (UK custodial model — landlord never holds it). Pooled float = permanent funding leg; deposit principal returns at move-out through adjudication; clean years earn the renter a claims-free dividend on top of the statutory 1% — declared from pool surplus when the books run ahead (mutual model, not a guaranteed payment). (AMENDED 10/23 — supersedes "reward after clean year".)

MN Renter's Credit (M1PR) = the ongoing renter-side share, structured as an advance payment — paid monthly to the pool, reconciled on the tax return (federal APTC mechanism, not a rent intercept). Enrollment rides the mandatory CRP e-file data rail; over-cap renters pay the share directly (~$6/mo) — disclosed, not hidden.

Landlord matched share = explicit third leg (merged 10/19 — "the other half"), PLUS the deposit float they no longer hold. Priced on the program base + experience surcharge; clean-book years earn a no-claim dividend, declared from surplus (added 10/23 — answers the median-landlord "it's a tax" attack).

Split ratio = open drafting question pending actuarial. The "Who pays" concept sheet was reconciled 10/19. The frozen model's tenant-premium figures still need reconciling in the next live version (sources stay frozen).
