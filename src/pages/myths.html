Myths & facts

Common claims about the bill — each answered, each sourced.

Three labels: fact = what the draft text or a named source says; model = a figure from the working math, still being verified; opinion = our read, labeled as such.

"It's rent control."fact

No. Nothing in the draft caps, sets, or limits rent. It is an insurance-style pool for what happens when things go wrong — deposits, verified hardship, and claims — not a price rule. Providers keep the right to charge and earn what the market carries.

Source: the live draft — eight locked clauses, none touching rent levels.

"It's a tax on landlords."model

It is structured as mutual insurance, not a tax: the matched share (modeled ≈ $6/unit/mo) buys real coverage — covered rent during verified hardship, deposit adjudication — and clean-book years earn a no-claim dividend declared from surplus. The honest cost is the deposit float providers no longer hold; the draft says that openly instead of hiding it.

Source: working model & locked decisions, the numbers and version history (10/23 entries).

"Renters end up paying for it anyway."model

Partly, and it is disclosed rather than hidden: the renter-side share (modeled ≈ $6/mo) rides the MN Renter's Credit as a monthly advance reconciled on the tax return — the federal APTC mechanism, not a rent intercept. Renters over the credit's income cap pay the share directly. In return they get the claims-free dividend, the deposit back through adjudication instead of a fight, and hardship coverage that no current product gives them without clawback.

Source: live draft funding section; M1PR figures — MN Dept. of Revenue TY2025.

"It lets bad tenants skip rent."fact

No. Hardship triggers are verified, coverage is capped per lease, claims history prices the renewal, and cooperation with a repayment plan on rent accruing after coverage is a condition of staying covered. Manufactured claims carry a surcharge plus referral. The design rule, locked in writing: tenant-side abuse controls must be as real as the landlord-side penalties.

Source: live draft, clause 5 and the fairness axiom in the decision log (10/19).

"The insurer could turn fraud reports into criminal charges against tenants."fact

Private parties cannot file charges — only prosecutors can. The draft adds three controls anyway: a penalty for knowingly-false fraud referrals (symmetric to the manufactured-claim penalty), tenant notice and a defense window before any referral transmits, and a defined evidence threshold. Claims data goes on a public registry so abuse is visible.

Source: live draft, carrier-abuse controls (10/19).

opinion Corruption can't be designed to zero — so the draft designs for it to be visible (registry), expensive (penalties), and appealable (independent review).

"The deposit disappears into a state fund."fact

The principal stays the renter's. Deposits sit in bonded custodial escrow for the tenancy — the same UK model operating since 2007 — and return at move-out through independent adjudication. The statutory 1% interest still accrues to the tenant. Only the float above that 1% funds the pool.

Source: live draft funding section; Minn. Stat. § 504B.178; UK Tenancy Deposit Protection precedent.

"Deposit-insurance products already do this."fact

Private deposit-replacement products (sold at roughly 3–7% of annual rent) pay the landlord and then recover from the tenant — they are surety products, which creates new debt rather than protection. France's Visale guarantee works the same way: clawback, and no deposit coverage. This bill's no-subrogation clause is the deliberate difference: a paid claim ends the matter.

Source: verification pass 10/23 — Visale clawback confirmed; SDR pricing from the working model's competitive survey.

"It's basically law already."fact

It is a working draft. Not filed, not before any legislature, no sponsor yet — and it may never become law. That is stated on every page because overclaiming status would be the easiest mistake to make and the hardest to take back.

Source: status line on the live draft — restated there every time the draft updates.
