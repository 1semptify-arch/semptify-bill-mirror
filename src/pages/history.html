Before the public record — the decision log

The draft predates its public repo. The design calls that shaped it are kept, dated, in the canon folder's decision log — the short version:

  • Oct 14 — The eight locked clauses: no subrogation; independent adjudication + tenant counter-claim lane; tenant/consumer board seats; manufactured-claim penalties; public claims registry; sunset + year-3 report; landlord-paid premium (later amended); experience rating.
  • Oct 16 — Named: the Minnesota Rent Assurance & Deposit Protection Act, presented by Semptify.
  • Oct 18 — Funding model resolved: deposits to program escrow at signing; renter's credit carries the ongoing share. Third coverage leg added: renter's belongings.
  • Oct 19 — The three-source braid merged: deposit float + M1PR advance + an explicit landlord matched share. The fairness axiom locked: tenant-side controls as real as landlord-side ones.
  • Oct 23 — Deposit shape amended to custodial escrow for the tenancy (UK model) with a claims-free dividend; rating resolved to community base + bounded experience surcharge; a real math defect found in audit and corrected in the open.
