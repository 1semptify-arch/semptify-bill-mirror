Corrections policy

How a figure earns its place. Every number on this site goes through three checks before it publishes: the source exists and says what we claim, the math is re-derived independently, and the figure is presented without spin. Fail any check and the figure waits — marked as still being verified, never asserted.

When we're wrong. A correction is made in the open: the figure changes, and the change itself stays visible — every edit to the draft and these pages is on the public version history. We do not quietly edit mistakes away.

To report an error. The fastest path is a note on the public board — answered in the open — or the contact page.

Fact sheet

What it is. The Minnesota Rent Assurance & Deposit Protection Act — a working draft for an insurance-style pool covering residential rental risk: custodial deposit protection, rent assurance during verified hardship, and a deferred belongings coverage leg. Written in public since October 2026.

Who is behind it. Semptify — a Minnesota tenant rights advocacy organization. Donation-funded, open source, pre-nonprofit (filing in progress). No advertising anywhere on the site, ever.

Status. Working draft. Not filed, no sponsor, not before any legislature — and it may never become law.
